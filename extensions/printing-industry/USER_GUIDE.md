# Printing Industry Pack — User Guide

## Installation

1. Install from Marketplace: `lateen marketplace install printing-industry`
2. Or install locally: `lateen extensions install extensions/printing-industry`
3. Enable: `lateen extensions enable printing-industry`

## What You Get

After installation, your organization receives pre-configured templates for:

- **20 product types** — from business cards to exhibition booths
- **12 machine types** — UV, latex, laser, CNC, and more
- **14 materials** — substrates and consumables
- **8 workflows** — quotation through warranty
- **6 missions** — production optimization, waste reduction, etc.
- **6 AI workers** — planners, schedulers, inspectors
- **6 dashboards** — production, sales, machines, warehouse, finance, CEO
- **6 reports** — production, machine, profit, material, downtime, installation

## Business DNA Setup

The pack includes organization and department templates:

- Production, Design, Sales, Installation, Warehouse, Quality, Finance

Import templates into Business DNA Studio after installation.
