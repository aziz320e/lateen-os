# Customer Insights

The **Customer Insights** capability within Intelligence analyzes customer behavior and segmentation.

## Responsibility

- Segment customers based on behavior, value, and lifecycle stage
- Identify churn risk, upsell opportunities, and satisfaction patterns
- Produce insight reports from Business DNA customer records and domain activity
- Feed findings to Sales AI, Marketing AI, and the customers domain

## Boundaries

Customer Insights owns _behavioral analysis_. Business DNA owns canonical customer records; the customers domain manages relationships and support.
