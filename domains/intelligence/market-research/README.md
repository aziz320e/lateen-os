# Market Research

The **Market Research** capability within Intelligence performs systematic analysis of markets and segments.

## Responsibility

- Analyze market size, growth, segmentation, and dynamics
- Produce research briefs aligned with Business DNA goals and products
- Integrate external data sources with internal Business DNA context
- Support strategic decisions with structured market evidence

## Boundaries

Market Research owns _market understanding_. Competitor intelligence focuses on rivals; sales and marketing domains execute go-to-market actions.
