# Recommendation Engine

The **Recommendation Engine** capability within Intelligence delivers contextual suggestions across Lateen OS.

## Responsibility

- Generate recommendations for products, actions, workflows, and resources
- Personalize suggestions based on role, domain, and Business DNA context
- Rank and explain recommendations for AI Workforce agents and human users
- Learn from outcomes to improve recommendation quality over time

## Boundaries

The recommendation engine owns _suggestion generation_. AI Workforce agents and business domains decide whether to act on recommendations.
