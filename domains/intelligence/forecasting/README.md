# Forecasting

The **Forecasting** capability within Intelligence produces predictive models for business outcomes.

## Responsibility

- Forecast demand, revenue, capacity, and operational metrics
- Align forecasts with Business DNA KPIs and goals
- Provide confidence intervals and scenario variants
- Supply predictions to Finance AI, Operations AI, and CEO AI

## Boundaries

Forecasting owns _predictive modeling_. Finance owns budgets and reporting; business domains execute against forecasts.
