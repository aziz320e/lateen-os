# Intelligence

The **Intelligence** domain provides analytical and discovery capabilities across Lateen OS.

Intelligence transforms raw data from Business DNA, business domains, and external sources into insights, forecasts, and recommendations. It feeds the AI Workforce and human operators with actionable intelligence — it does not execute business actions directly.

## Capabilities

| Capability                                            | Responsibility                                             |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| [trend-discovery](./trend-discovery/)                 | Detecting emerging patterns and market shifts              |
| [market-research](./market-research/)                 | Systematic analysis of markets and segments                |
| [competitor-intelligence](./competitor-intelligence/) | Monitoring and analyzing competitor activity               |
| [product-discovery](./product-discovery/)             | Identifying product opportunities and gaps                 |
| [machine-discovery](./machine-discovery/)             | Finding automation and integration opportunities           |
| [pricing-intelligence](./pricing-intelligence/)       | Price analysis, benchmarking, and optimization signals     |
| [customer-insights](./customer-insights/)             | Behavioral analysis and customer segmentation              |
| [knowledge-mining](./knowledge-mining/)               | Extracting structured insights from unstructured knowledge |
| [forecasting](./forecasting/)                         | Predictive models for demand, revenue, and operations      |
| [recommendation-engine](./recommendation-engine/)     | Contextual suggestions across domains and workflows        |

## Boundaries

Intelligence owns _analysis and discovery_. It consumes Business DNA and domain data; it does not own business entities or execute workflows. The AI Workforce acts on intelligence outputs; business domains remain the source of truth.
