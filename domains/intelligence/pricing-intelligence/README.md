# Pricing Intelligence

The **Pricing Intelligence** capability within Intelligence analyzes pricing dynamics and optimization signals.

## Responsibility

- Benchmark prices against market and competitor data
- Analyze margin, elasticity, and revenue impact of pricing changes
- Recommend pricing adjustments aligned with Business DNA policies and KPIs
- Support Sales AI and Finance AI with pricing context

## Boundaries

Pricing Intelligence owns _pricing analysis_. Business DNA policies govern pricing rules; sales and finance domains execute pricing decisions.
