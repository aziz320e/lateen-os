# Competitor Intelligence

The **Competitor Intelligence** capability within Intelligence monitors and analyzes competitor activity.

## Responsibility

- Track competitor products, pricing, positioning, and announcements
- Compare competitor capabilities against Business DNA products and services
- Generate competitive briefs and threat assessments
- Alert relevant AI Workforce agents and business domains to significant changes

## Boundaries

Competitor Intelligence owns _rival analysis_. Pricing intelligence handles price optimization; marketing and sales domains act on competitive insights.
