# Product Discovery

The **Product Discovery** capability within Intelligence identifies product opportunities and gaps.

## Responsibility

- Analyze customer demand, market gaps, and product performance signals
- Recommend new products, features, or retirements based on evidence
- Cross-reference Business DNA product catalog with intelligence findings
- Feed discovery outputs to Product Manager AI and the products domain

## Boundaries

Product Discovery owns _opportunity identification_. The products domain and Business DNA own the canonical product catalog; R&D AI evaluates feasibility.
