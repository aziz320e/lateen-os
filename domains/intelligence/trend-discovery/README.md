# Trend Discovery

The **Trend Discovery** capability within Intelligence detects emerging patterns and shifts relevant to the organization.

## Responsibility

- Monitor internal and external signals for new trends
- Classify trends by domain, urgency, and business impact
- Surface trend reports to the AI Workforce and business domains
- Track trend lifecycle from emergence through adoption or decline

## Boundaries

Trend Discovery owns _pattern detection_. Market research validates trends in context; business domains decide whether to act on them.
