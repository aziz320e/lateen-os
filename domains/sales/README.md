# Sales

The **Sales** domain manages the commercial process of converting interest into revenue.

## Responsibility

- Define and track the sales pipeline and deal stages
- Manage proposals, quotations, and commercial negotiations
- Forecast revenue and monitor sales performance
- Govern pricing rules and commercial terms at the point of sale

## Boundaries

This domain owns _how revenue is won_. It does not own brand messaging (marketing), financial recording (finance), or long-term customer success (customers).
