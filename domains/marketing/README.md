# Marketing

The **Marketing** domain manages how the organization communicates its value to the market.

## Responsibility

- Define brand identity, messaging, and positioning
- Plan and track campaigns, channels, and audience engagement
- Manage content strategy and market-facing communications
- Measure awareness, reach, and marketing performance

## Boundaries

This domain owns _how we are perceived_ and _how we attract attention_. It does not own deal closure (sales), product definition (products), or customer support (customers).
