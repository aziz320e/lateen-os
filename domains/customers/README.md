# Customers

The **Customers** domain manages relationships with the organization's clients and users.

## Responsibility

- Maintain the canonical customer registry and contact records
- Track the customer lifecycle from acquisition through retention
- Manage support interactions, feedback, and satisfaction signals
- Govern customer segmentation, health scores, and engagement history

## Boundaries

This domain owns _who we serve and how we relate to them_. It does not own lead generation (marketing), deal closure (sales), or product definition (products).
