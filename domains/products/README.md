# Products

The **Products** domain manages what the organization offers to the market.

## Responsibility

- Define the product catalog, tiers, and feature sets
- Govern product lifecycle from conception through retirement
- Maintain product documentation, specifications, and roadmaps
- Coordinate product-market fit signals across domains

## Boundaries

This domain owns _what we sell and deliver_. It does not own campaign execution (marketing), deal closure (sales), or customer relationship management (customers).
