# Finance

The **Finance** domain manages the organization's financial health and accountability.

## Responsibility

- Maintain budgets, forecasts, and financial plans
- Track income, expenses, assets, and liabilities
- Produce financial reports and compliance records
- Govern fiscal policies, approvals, and audit trails

## Boundaries

This domain owns _how money is planned, tracked, and reported_. It does not own deal negotiation (sales), product pricing strategy (products), or operational workflows (operations).
