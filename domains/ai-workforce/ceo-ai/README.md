# CEO AI

The **CEO AI** agent provides strategic oversight and cross-domain coordination within Lateen OS.

## Responsibility

- Synthesize intelligence across all domains into executive-level briefings
- Monitor Business DNA KPIs and goals against actual performance
- Coordinate priorities across AI Workforce agents and business domains
- Escalate decisions that require human executive approval

## Boundaries

CEO AI owns _strategic coordination_. It does not modify Business DNA directly without authorization; domain agents execute operational work.
