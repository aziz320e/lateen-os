# Finance AI

The **Finance AI** agent supports financial planning, analysis, and compliance.

## Responsibility

- Analyze financial data against Business DNA budgets, KPIs, and goals
- Support forecasting with intelligence from the forecasting capability
- Monitor compliance with Business DNA financial policies
- Recommend cost optimization and investment priorities

## Boundaries

Finance AI owns _financial analysis and recommendations_. The finance domain maintains accounts and reports; Business DNA owns policies and KPI definitions.
