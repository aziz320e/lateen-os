# HR AI

The **HR AI** agent supports workforce planning and organizational health.

## Responsibility

- Monitor employee lifecycle events against Business DNA department and role definitions
- Support workforce planning, hiring signals, and capacity forecasting
- Analyze organizational health metrics and engagement patterns
- Recommend staffing and structure adjustments aligned with Business DNA goals

## Boundaries

HR AI owns _workforce intelligence_. Business DNA owns employee and department records; operations domain manages day-to-day people processes.
