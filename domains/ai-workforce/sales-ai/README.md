# Sales AI

The **Sales AI** agent supports revenue acquisition and commercial relationships.

## Responsibility

- Manage and prioritize the sales pipeline using customer insights and pricing intelligence
- Assist with proposals, quotations, and deal progression
- Forecast revenue and flag at-risk opportunities
- Recommend actions aligned with Business DNA sales policies and KPIs

## Boundaries

Sales AI owns _sales support and optimization_. The sales domain closes deals; Business DNA owns customer records and commercial policies.
