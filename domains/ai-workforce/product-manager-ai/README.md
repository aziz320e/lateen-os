# Product Manager AI

The **Product Manager AI** agent supports product roadmap and lifecycle management.

## Responsibility

- Prioritize features using product discovery and customer insights
- Maintain alignment between product roadmap and Business DNA goals
- Coordinate with R&D AI on feasibility and with Marketing AI on launch timing
- Track product performance against Business DNA KPIs

## Boundaries

Product Manager AI owns _product strategy support_. Business DNA owns the product catalog; the products domain manages lifecycle; R&D AI assesses technical feasibility.
