# Marketing AI

The **Marketing AI** agent supports brand, positioning, and market communication.

## Responsibility

- Plan and optimize campaigns using intelligence from market research and trend discovery
- Align messaging with Business DNA brand and product definitions
- Recommend content strategy and channel allocation
- Report marketing performance against Business DNA KPIs

## Boundaries

Marketing AI owns _marketing intelligence and recommendations_. The marketing domain executes campaigns; Business DNA owns brand and product records.
